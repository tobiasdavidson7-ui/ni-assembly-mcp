Search what was actually said in the Assembly.

Find the words behind the votes: full debates, questions and answers, and motions, alongside the members and votes themselves — straight from the official records.

Would you rather ask questions in plain English instead of searching these records yourself? Link them to your own AI.

What you get here

  • Full-text search of debates (Hansard)
  • Search of parliamentary questions and their answers
  • Motions, amendments and related bills
  • Recorded votes (divisions) and member details

This is not a tool for tracking attendance or votes, rather it offers insight into the substance of the debate. We hope you find it useful!

Example Questions (and where to look)

  • What did someone say about a topic in a debate? Search contributions →
  • Has this issue been raised before? Search parliamentary questions →
  • What was a minister's answer to a question about X? Search parliamentary questions →
  • How did a member vote on something? Recorded votes (divisions) →
  • What's happening at the Assembly this week? Business diary →
  • What does a committee cover? List all committees →

No account, no sign-up. This site isn't run by the Assembly and never puts a language model between you and the record — unless you bring your own.
